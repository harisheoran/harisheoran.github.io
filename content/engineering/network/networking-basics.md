Terms
1. Bandwidth: Max Capacity of network link to transfer data over time
2. PPS: Packet per second

**The Math**: Packet rate depends directly on your bandwidth capacity and the average size of the packets being sent. Processing millions of tiny packets strains network routers and switches much more than moving a few giant packets, even at identical bandwidth consumption
